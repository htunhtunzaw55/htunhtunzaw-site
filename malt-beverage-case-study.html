A seasonal campaign.
Three channels.
One connected plan.

Building awareness and engagement for a malt beverage brand through Facebook, TikTok and programmatic rich media.

Client
Malt beverage brand · Anonymous
Market & season
Myanmar · Festive campaign, 2026
My role
Performance Marketer
01 / The brief

From visibility
to participation.

The campaign supported seasonal gifting with brand content, creator content and user-generated content activities. The challenge was to build broad awareness while encouraging people to watch, interact and take part.

I managed paid media across Facebook, TikTok and Eskimi rich media, aligning campaign objectives and budgets with awareness, video engagement and participation goals.

02 / My contribution

Ownership across
the media workflow.

  • Media planning & budget allocationPlanned channel and objective allocations around campaign goals.
  • Campaign setup & targetingBuilt campaigns and configured audiences across the three platforms.
  • Daily optimizationMonitored spend, delivery and creative performance, making ongoing campaign adjustments.
  • Reporting & recommendationsEvaluated results by objective and translated findings into next-campaign recommendations.

KOL coordination was handled separately; my role covered paid media management.

03 / The approach

Give each channel a clear job.

01

Facebook

Reach, video views and interaction objectives supported different campaign needs. The microsite activity launched with Reach; Engagement was added after encouraging early results.

02

TikTok

Separate promotions supported video views, engagement and follower growth. I compared short festive edits and creator videos against their intended objectives.

03

Eskimi rich media

Programmatic placements extended awareness beyond social platforms. Reporting considered impressions, clicks, frequency and viewability together.

05 / What I learned

Turn delivery into
better decisions.

Match creative to the objective.

Short festive video edits delivered efficient views and interactions. Selected creator content showed stronger focused viewing. Video length and campaign purpose matter when comparing those results.

Scale and quality need separate checks.

Programmatic delivery exceeded its impression target by approximately 60%, while viewability was 33.67%. The next opportunity is to improve placement quality alongside delivery efficiency.

Connect engagement to participation.

UGC participation provided a useful campaign outcome, but stronger tracking is needed to establish which paid activity drove entries. That is a measurement priority for the next campaign.
