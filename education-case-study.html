01 / The challenge

worth following up.

The campaign aimed to generate education enquiries that progressed into warm leads: prospects who shared a phone number or email for follow-up.

I contributed as a Performance Marketing Executive within the team, supporting paid campaigns across study abroad services and other education programs. The results on this page cover the overall education campaign, rather than study abroad alone.

05 / Takeaways

Track what happens
after the message.

Use a clear lead definition.

A shared phone number or email gave the campaign a concrete follow-up milestone. Tracking that outcome alongside message starts made the reporting more useful.

Read cost and progression together.

Cost per message describes the price of starting a conversation. Cost per warm lead shows what it takes to reach the next stage. Both help assess campaign efficiency.

Keep the attribution clear.

The reported outcomes reflect the combined campaign and team effort. My contribution covered campaign execution, strategy, budgets, optimization, testing and reporting.
